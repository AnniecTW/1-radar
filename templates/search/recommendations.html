Embedding ranking vs simple rules

{{ row.label }}{{ cell.value|floatformat:2|default:"—" }}

Hit rate: students with at least one event they want in their top 10. Recall@10: share of the events they want that made the top 10. Precision@4: share of the first 4 cards that they want, with empty slots counted as misses so a shorter row can’t score higher. A student who wants only 2 events can reach at most 0.5, the same for every method, so compare methods rather than read it as a grade. Coverage: share of test events recommended to anyone. Interest tags shows events in the chosen categories; Soonest first shows everyone the same events. Borderline events, and events a student already RSVP’d to, saved, liked or viewed, are left out of these scores. Hidden event: each RSVP or save is removed in turn (the recommender hides those events) to see whether the event comes back in the top 10; MRR is 1 / its position, or 0.
